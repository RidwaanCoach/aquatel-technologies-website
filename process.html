The basics

What is reverse osmosis?

Reverse osmosis (RO) is a purification method that pushes water under pressure through a fine semi-permeable membrane. The membrane allows water molecules through while reducing dissolved solids, salts and the majority of contaminants.

Combined with pre- and post-filtration, RO produces water that is clean, low in dissolved solids, and pleasant to drink.
